WHO WE ARE

For the welfare of all, and the peace of the world.

Vishwakalyan Shanti Foundation (VKSF) is a social-welfare initiative built around a simple belief: meaningful change begins when people come together with compassion, responsibility and a spirit of service.

Through 25 focused communities, the foundation aims to encourage awareness, participation and action across social, environmental, educational and humanitarian causes.
